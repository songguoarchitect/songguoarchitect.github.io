I'm now a Postdoctoral Fellow at [MIT SENSEable City Lab](https://senseable.mit.edu/). I earned my Bachelor, Master and Ph.D. in Architecture at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).

My research focuses on applying AI to 1)urban analytics, 2)architectural studies, and 3)digital heritages.My research experience spans topics related to [human well-being](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42949-025-00306-9), [place identity](https://senseable.mit.edu/japan-uniqueness/), and [people's perception](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0926580526001226).

My architectural works mainly focus on large-scale cultural and educational public buildings. The key projects I have contributed to include:
<br>[McCourt School of Public Policy](https://mccourt.georgetown.edu/life-at-mccourt/) ([RAMSA,New York](https://www.ramsa.com/), in use); 
<br>[Nanhu Biological Laboratory](http://www.archina.com/index.php?g=works&m=index&a=show&id=16325) ([THAD](https://www.thad.com.cn/), in use),etc.

I enjoy working with ceramics, watercolor and photography in my free time.
