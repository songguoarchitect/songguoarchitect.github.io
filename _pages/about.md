I earned my Bachelor, Master and Ph.D.(this October) in Architecture at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).I'm also a former visiting Ph.D. researcher and research lead at [MIT SENSEable City Lab](https://senseable.mit.edu/).

My research focuses on applying AI to 1)urban analytics, 2)architectural studies, and 3)digital heritages.My research experience spans topics related to [human well-being](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-6235999/v1), [place identity](https://senseable.mit.edu/japan-uniqueness/),and [people's perception](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10732175).

My architectural works mainly focus on large-scale cultural and educational public buildings. The key projects I have contributed to include:
<br>[McCourt School of Public Policy](https://mccourt.georgetown.edu/life-at-mccourt/) ([RAMSA,New York](https://www.ramsa.com/), in use); 
<br>[Nanhu Biological Laboratory](http://www.archina.com/index.php?g=works&m=index&a=show&id=16325) ([THAD](https://www.thad.com.cn/), in use),etc.

I enjoy working with ceramics, watercolor and photography in my free time.
